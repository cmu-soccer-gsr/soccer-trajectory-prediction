A better reader: Koshkina & Elder

The released jersey pipeline of Koshkina & Elder run on the cached baseline's own tracklets for 5 validation clips (SNGS-021 to 025). Their numbers replace MMOCR's in a copy of the state and nothing else changes, so the GS-HOTA difference comes from the reader alone.

1 · LegibilityA classifier drops frames where the number cannot be read.
2 · Torso cropViTPose finds the shoulders and hips and crops the back or chest.
3 · ReadPARSeq, a scene-text recognizer, reads the digits in each crop.
4 · VoteReads are combined over the tracklet, weighted by confidence.

What would move the number

Swapping the reader was the biggest lever, as the MMOCR breakdown suggested. What is left after Koshkina & Elder:

Run all 58 clipsThe +6.06 comes from 5 clips. Scoring the full validation split makes it comparable with the 58-clip MMOCR numbers and shows whether the gain holds.
Tune what counts as readableHalf of the labeled tracklets still end with no number. The legibility classifier and the vote threshold (left at their default of 1.0) decide this, and their ReID outlier filter was not switched on.
Adapt to these clipsNumbers are about 14 px tall and often blurred. Fine-tuning the legibility or reading step on SoccerNet crops, or picking the sharpest frames first, targets the remaining misses.
