What would move the number

Each failure bucket points to a different fix, sized by how many tracklets it covers.

A reader that finds the digits76% of scored tracklets get no number although one is labeled. Most of the gain is here: a stronger digit reader, or a step that first picks the frames where the back is visible.
Better crops and frame choice11% are read wrong in every frame. Small boxes, motion blur and partial digits (70 share a digit with the truth) suggest feeding larger, sharper crops.
Smarter voting across framesOnly 2% had the right number in some frame and lost the vote, so a learned aggregation alone moves little until the reader improves.
