How the model works

One forward pass of the possession model for the frame on screen. Every box shows this frame's values, so the numbers change as play goes on.

1 · Read the feed

What the model sees at this frame
Player on the ball–last 2 s of positions (20 frames)
Ball–last 2 s, with height
Everyone else–position, speed, team, goalkeeper, on camera
Turn the pitchPut the carrier at the centre and make their team attack to the right, so every situation looks alike to the model.

2 · Encode

Turn each input into a vector of 96 numbers
GRU, 2 layersReads the carrier's and the ball's 2 s, frame by frame, and sums up how the carrier is moving with the ball.
Small network per playerOne vector per other player from where they are relative to the carrier and how they move.

3 · Compare players

Transformer, 2 layers, 4 attention heads
Attention–Every player's vector looks at every other one: who is open, who is marked, who is in the passing lane. This is where context enters.

4 · Predict

Four outputs, trained together
How the possession ends–
Lets go within 1 s–
Who gets a pass–one score per teammate, they add up to 100%
Does it arrive–

5 · Draw

What you see on the board
Yellow arrow–= chance of a pass × chance this teammate gets it. Drawn when 5% or more.
Time taken–300k parameters, one CPU thread, budget 100 ms per frame
